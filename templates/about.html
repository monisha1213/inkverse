About InkVerse

InkVerse is a story and novel publishing platform where writers can share their stories chapter by chapter, and readers can discover, follow, and engage with their favorite authors — inspired by platforms like Pratilipi.

Features

  • Write and publish stories across multiple genres and languages
  • Serialized chapters with a clean reading experience
  • Likes, bookmarks, comments, and author follows
  • Search and filter by genre and language

Built With

Python, Flask, SQLAlchemy, SQLite, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

About the Creator

InkVerse was built by Monisha as a full-stack learning project, covering authentication, relational databases, file uploads, and social features from the ground up.
